# 📊 Blinkit Grocery Sales & Outlet Analysis Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview
This repository features an interactive **Excel Data Analysis Dashboard** designed to evaluate and optimize the sales performance of **Blinkit** outlets across India. By processing a comprehensive dataset of 8,523 items, this project uncovers hidden retail trends, compares structural performance metrics, and translates granular transactional operational metrics into actionable business intelligence.

Developed by: **Akash**

## 🎯 Project Objectives & Problem Statement
The primary goal is to conduct a holistic performance analysis of Blinkit's operational ecosystem to identify areas for financial growth and inventory efficiency. 

* **Identify Key Sales Drivers:** Isolate structural and item characteristics influencing top-line revenue.
* **Assess Outlet Formats:** Compare sales across small, medium, and large store profiles alongside geographical tier segments.
* **Optimize Assortment Strategy:** Track customer demand variations regarding item types and fat content variations.
* **Balance Operations & Quality:** Monitor how customer ratings correlate with revenue performance to ensure continuous service satisfaction.

## 🔑 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) Captured
The core business health of Blinkit's retail grid is monitored using four macro metrics tracked in the high-level dashboard panel:
* 💰 **Total Sales:** **$1.20M** in historical gross revenue generated across all operating channels.
* 📈 **Average Sales:** **$141** generated per item transaction, indicating consumer purchasing bandwidth.
* 📦 **Number of Items:** **8,523** distinct inventory products actively distributed through the grid.
* ⭐ **Average Rating:** **4.0 / 5.0**, representing standard customer satisfaction and feedback thresholds.

## 🔍 Deep-Dive Analysis Dimensions
The dashboard breaks down performance across distinct core transactional segments:

1. **Product Performance (`Item Type`):** Dissects contribution margins among product groups. Top performers include **Fruits and Vegetables ($178.1K)** and **Snack Foods ($175.4K)**.
2. **Health Preferences (`Fat Content`):** Tracks consumer lifestyle purchases, revealing a higher preference for **Low Fat products (65% of Total Sales / $776.3K)** compared to Regular products (35%).
3. **Store Footprint (`Outlet Size`):** Compares physical infrastructure efficiency. **Medium-sized outlets generate the highest market share at 42% ($507.9K)**, followed by High/Large format stores at 37%.
4. **Geographic Demographics (`Outlet Location`):** Analyzes target demographics across Indian city tiers. **Tier 3 locations lead gross revenue generation with $472.1K**.
5. **Business Format (`Outlet Type`):** Compares localized quick-commerce models like Grocery Stores against standard Supermarket formats.
6. **Timeline Trends (`Outlet Establishment`):** Visualizes revenue patterns based on establishment year, peaking dramatically in **2018 at $204.5K**.

## 🛠️ Tools & Skills Utilized
* **Primary Platform:** Microsoft Excel
* **Data Processing & ETL:** Power Query (Data cleaning, handling mixed casing, and null value resolution).
* **Analytical Computation:** Pivot Tables, Advanced Excel formulas (`SUMIFS`, `AVERAGEIFS`, `VLOOKUP`).
* **UI/UX Design:** Custom Canvas layout layout featuring corporate yellow branding elements, visual metric indicators, and modern typography.
* **Interactive Controls:** Advanced **Slicers** connected across multiple pivot tables for real-time data drilling (`Outlet Size`, `Outlet Location Tier`, `Item Type`).


## 📈 Strategic Business Recommendations Based on Data
Based on the dashboard insights, the following strategic actions can optimize operations:
* **Inventory Allocation:** Prioritize high-performing inventory categories like *Fruits & Vegetables* and *Snack Foods* in stock fulfillment pipelines.
* **Expansion Blueprint:** Replicate the structural framework of **Medium-sized Supermarket Type 1 formats**, which generate the most stable transaction metrics.
* **Regional Optimization:** Focus hyper-local marketing strategies on **Tier 3 cities** to extract maximum localized value from high-performing geographic hubs.
* **Quality Assurance:** Flag individual outlet segments dipping below the global **4.0 rating baseline** to address customer retention hurdles before revenue decays.
